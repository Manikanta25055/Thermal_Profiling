## VERIFY BEFORE BUYING

### DS18B20 Authenticity
- Check reviews mention "genuine Maxim chip"
- Avoid suspiciously cheap listings (<₹50)
- Waterproof probe should have stainless steel casing
- Cable should be ~1 meter with 3 wires

### USB-RS485 Adapter
- Chipset: CH340, FTDI, or CP2102
- macOS driver support mentioned
- Terminal type: Screw terminals preferred
- Status LEDs: TX/RX indicators helpful
